## Os 5 tópicos (cada átomo tem de ser TREINADO por ≥1 produto)

### T1. Fairy tales — story elements (S4)
Fairy tale = **a make-believe type of story**.
- **Characters:** royalty (king, queen, princess, prince, knight); magical creatures (fairies, dragons, ogres); **good character × bad character**; **a special helper** (fairy, godmother, wizard, elf); **talking animals**.
- **Settings:** kingdom (castle, forest, village), house.
- **Story elements:** magical objects (wand, books, foods); **special numbers: threes or sevens**; **beginning – middle – end**.
- **Story events:** special beginning **"Once upon a time…"**; special ending **"And they lived happily ever after."**; **spells, enchantments, curses = magic**; **a lesson or moral**.
Cobrança provável: classificar em characters / settings / events; identificar o começo e o final especiais; quem é o special helper; números especiais (3 e 7); o que é magia; fairy tale é real ou make-believe.

### T2. Real or make-believe (S2) — já visto no 2º tri (ELA Unit 4), volta agora
- **Real:** a thing, character or story that really exists or can really happen in real life. We can see, touch, or it is something that is TRUE. Ex.: a dog, a tree, the school, the sun, the story of Anne Frank.
- **Make-believe:** a thing, character or story that is NOT real or true and cannot really happen in real life. We can't touch it; it is imagined or invented. Ex.: superheroes, talking animals, Cinderella, fairy tales.
- Imagens do slide: Harry Potter (make-believe), jacaré (real), jogador de futebol Richarlison (real), Mew/Pokémon (make-believe), *O diário de Anne Frank* (real), *Diary of a Wimpy Kid* (make-believe), jacaré debaixo da cama (ilustração de livro: make-believe).
Armadilha: o jacaré é real, mas "um jacaré que fala/mora debaixo da cama" é make-believe.

### T3. Circle story (S1, S6)
- A circle story **begins and ends in the same place or with the same thing**. **Many events** happen in the middle, but at the end the story **comes back to the beginning**.
- Exemplo do material: *If You Give a Mouse a Cookie* (Laura Numeroff): começa com o biscoito e termina com o biscoito de novo. A roda da sala tem 14 eventos (cookie → milk → straw → napkin → mirror → scissors → broom → nap → story → drawing → pen → tape/refrigerator → milk → cookie).
- Beginning: *the story starts with a cookie*; middle: *many events happen*; end: *the story ends with a cookie again*.
Cobrança provável: definir circle story; dizer com o que começa e termina; reconhecer que uma história é ou não circular; pôr eventos na ordem.

### T4. Cause and effect (P, S6)
- **Cause and effect happen when one thing makes another thing happen.**
- Cause = **"If…"**; Effect = **"Then…"**.
- Exemplos do material: *If Ash goes on adventure… → Then he will find many Pokémon to catch.* / *If Cinderella goes to the ball… → Then she will lose one of her slippers.* / *If you read books every day… → Then you will be more intelligent and learn new things.* / *If you give a dog a donut → Then he'll want to drink some juice.*
- Cause and Effect Chart (book title; If… / Then…), ligado a *If You Give a Mouse a Cookie*.
Cobrança provável: ligar cause ↔ effect; dizer qual parte é a causa e qual é o efeito; completar "If… Then…".

### T5. Poetry and poem; elements of a poem (S3, S5)
- **Poetry = the full art. Poem = the text/paper.**
- Elements of a poem: **title**, **lines**, **stanza (is a group of lines)**, **author**. Exemplo: o poema *animals* (Brian Bilston) tem **31 lines** e **8 stanzas**.
- Other elements: **Rhyme** — poems often have words that sound the same at the end of the lines (cat / hat). **Rhythm** — poems can have a beat or a pattern when we read them; they often sound like a song. **Imagery** — poems use words to help us create pictures in our minds.
Cobrança provável: apontar título, autor, contar lines e stanzas de um poema curto; achar a rima; definir stanza; poetry × poem; rhyme × rhythm × imagery.

## Armadilhas de L2 (treinar explicitamente)
- **poem × poetry** (o texto × a arte inteira).
- **line × stanza** (uma linha × grupo de linhas).
- **rhyme × rhythm** (som igual no fim × batida).
- **cause × effect** (If = cause; Then = effect).
- **real × true × make-believe**.
